About Just Windows USA

Family-owned window company in Mentor, OH since 1992.

Just Windows USA serves Mentor, OH and surrounding communities with practical guidance, fair pricing, high-quality replacement windows, door installation, and professional service.

Local craftsmanship

Simple window and door service built around the homeowner.

Since 1992, Tom McPeek Jr. has been refining his skills in the window industry. With close attention to detail and a laid-back style of doing business, working with the crew is as simple as a home improvement project can be.

For affordable installations and repairs, Just Windows USA is the team to call for window replacement, entry doors, patio doors, and door replacement in Mentor and nearby areas.

Homeowners usually come to the team with practical questions: which window style fits the room, whether the existing frame can be reused, how long installation will take, and what warranty coverage looks like after the work is finished. The goal is to answer those questions before the project starts so the finished window or door upgrade feels straightforward from the first quote to the final walkthrough.

The company works with respected product lines such as Polaris, ProVia, and Andersen, while keeping the buying process local and personal. That combination gives customers access to strong window and door options without losing the direct communication that matters during a home improvement project.
