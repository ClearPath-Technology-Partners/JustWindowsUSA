Privacy policy.

What this website collects, how it is used, and how to reach us about it.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Just Windows USA ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect on this website and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Quote form submissions. If you request a quote through this site, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email, and project details, so we can respond to your inquiry.
  • Analytics. We use privacy-friendly website analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) to understand general traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and how people find our site. This does not identify you personally and does not use cookies.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry and provide the services you have requested. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party content

Some pages embed content from third-party services, such as Google Maps and Facebook. When those embeds load, the third party may receive standard technical information from your browser. Their own privacy policies apply to that content.

Your choices

If you would like us to delete information you submitted through this site, call us at (440) 668-4065 and we will take care of it.
